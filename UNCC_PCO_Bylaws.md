# Bylaws of the UNCC Pre-Law Club Organization (PCO)

**University of North Carolina at Charlotte**

**Adopted: July 24, 2026**

---

## Article I — Name

The name of this organization shall be the **UNCC Pre-Law Club Organization**, hereinafter referred to as "PCO" or "the Organization."

## Article II — Purpose

The purpose of the Organization shall be to:

1. Foster interest in law, legal studies, and the legal profession among students at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte.
2. Provide educational programming, networking opportunities, and resources to students pursuing careers in law.
3. Facilitate connections between students and legal professionals, law schools, and related institutions.
4. Promote civic engagement, legal literacy, and public service among members.
5. Support members in the law school application process, including LSAT preparation, personal statement development, and school selection.

## Article III — Membership

### Section 1 — Eligibility

Membership shall be open to all currently enrolled students at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte, regardless of major, classification, or background.

### Section 2 — Active Membership

Active members are those who have completed a membership application, paid any applicable dues, and participate in Organization activities. Only active members in good standing may vote in elections or hold office.

### Section 3 — Dues

Membership dues, if any, shall be determined by the Executive Board at the beginning of each academic year. The amount and payment schedule shall be communicated to all prospective and returning members.

### Section 4 — Non-Discrimination

The Organization shall not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, disability, age, veteran status, or any other protected classification.

## Article IV — Officers and Executive Board

### Section 1 — Composition

The Executive Board shall consist of the following officers:

1. **President**
2. **Vice President**
3. **Treasurer**
4. **Secretary**
5. Such additional officers as the Executive Board may create by majority vote.

### Section 2 — Qualifications

All officers must be active members in good standing and maintain a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.5 during their term of office.

### Section 3 — Terms of Office

Officers shall serve a term of one (1) academic year, commencing at the first meeting of the fall semester and concluding at the last meeting of the spring semester. Officers may be re-elected for consecutive terms.

### Section 4 — Duties of Officers

#### A. President

The President shall:
- Preside over all general and Executive Board meetings.
- Serve as the official representative and spokesperson of the Organization.
- Coordinate the activities of all officers and committees.
- Ensure compliance with University policies and these Bylaws.
- Possess authority to co-sign financial instruments on behalf of the Organization.
- Appoint committee chairs with the approval of the Executive Board.

#### B. Vice President

The Vice President shall:
- Assume the duties of the President in the President's absence or incapacity.
- Assist the President in the coordination of Organization activities.
- Oversee committee operations and report on their progress to the Executive Board.
- Serve as an authorized signatory on the Organization's financial accounts, including trust accounts.
- Coordinate membership recruitment and engagement efforts.

#### C. Treasurer

The Treasurer shall:
- Manage and maintain accurate records of all Organization finances.
- Prepare and present financial reports to the Executive Board and general membership.
- Collect dues and other revenues on behalf of the Organization.
- Disburse funds as authorized by the Executive Board.
- Maintain the Organization's financial accounts in accordance with University policy.
- Prepare an annual budget for approval by the Executive Board.

#### D. Secretary

The Secretary shall:
- Record and maintain minutes of all general and Executive Board meetings.
- Maintain the official records and correspondence of the Organization.
- Distribute meeting notices, agendas, and minutes to members.
- Maintain an accurate roster of all active members.

### Section 5 — Vacancies

In the event of a vacancy in any office other than the President, the Executive Board shall appoint a replacement by majority vote to serve the remainder of the term. A vacancy in the office of President shall be filled by the Vice President.

### Section 6 — Removal of Officers

An officer may be removed from office for cause by a two-thirds (2/3) vote of the active membership present at a meeting called for that purpose, provided that the officer has been given written notice of the charges and an opportunity to respond at least seven (7) days prior to the vote.

## Article V — Meetings

### Section 1 — General Meetings

General meetings of the Organization shall be held at least twice per month during the academic year, at a time and place determined by the Executive Board.

### Section 2 — Executive Board Meetings

The Executive Board shall meet at least once per month during the academic year. Special meetings may be called by the President or by a majority of the Executive Board.

### Section 3 — Quorum

A quorum for general meetings shall consist of one-third (1/3) of the active membership. A quorum for Executive Board meetings shall consist of a majority of the sitting officers.

### Section 4 — Voting

Each active member in good standing shall be entitled to one (1) vote on matters brought before the general membership. Decisions shall be made by a simple majority unless otherwise specified in these Bylaws.

## Article VI — Elections

### Section 1 — Timing

Elections for officers shall be held annually during the month of April.

### Section 2 — Nominations

Nominations shall be opened at least two (2) weeks prior to the election. Any active member in good standing who meets the qualifications set forth in Article IV, Section 2 may be nominated or self-nominate.

### Section 3 — Voting Procedure

Elections shall be conducted by secret ballot. The candidate receiving the greatest number of votes for each position shall be elected. In the event of a tie, a runoff election shall be held between the tied candidates.

## Article VII — Finances

### Section 1 — Fiscal Year

The fiscal year of the Organization shall run from August 1 through July 31.

### Section 2 — Financial Accounts

The Organization may establish and maintain bank accounts, trust accounts, and other financial accounts as necessary for the conduct of its business. All accounts shall be established in the name of the Organization and shall require dual authorization for withdrawals or disbursements exceeding two hundred fifty dollars ($250.00).

### Section 3 — Trust Accounts

The Organization may establish one or more trust accounts for the purpose of holding and managing funds designated for specific purposes, including but not limited to scholarships, event funding, and special projects. Trust accounts shall be administered in accordance with the following:

1. Trust accounts shall be established by resolution of the Executive Board.
2. Authorized signatories on trust accounts shall be designated by resolution of the Executive Board and shall include at minimum two (2) officers.
3. Funds held in trust shall be used solely for the purposes specified at the time of their designation.
4. The Treasurer shall maintain separate records for all trust account transactions.
5. Trust account activity shall be reported to the general membership at least once per semester.

### Section 4 — Disbursements

All expenditures shall be approved by the Executive Board. Expenditures exceeding five hundred dollars ($500.00) shall require approval by a majority vote of the general membership.

### Section 5 — Audit

The Executive Board shall arrange for a review of the Organization's financial records at least once per academic year.

## Article VIII — Committees

### Section 1 — Standing Committees

The Executive Board may establish standing committees as necessary to carry out the work of the Organization. Standing committees may include, but are not limited to:

1. Events and Programming Committee
2. Membership and Outreach Committee
3. Professional Development Committee
4. Community Service Committee

### Section 2 — Ad Hoc Committees

The President, with the approval of the Executive Board, may appoint ad hoc committees for specific tasks or projects. Ad hoc committees shall be dissolved upon completion of their assigned task.

### Section 3 — Committee Chairs

Each committee shall have a chair appointed by the President with the approval of the Executive Board. Committee chairs shall report regularly to the Executive Board.

## Article IX — Advisor

The Organization shall have at least one (1) faculty or staff advisor from the University of North Carolina at Charlotte. The advisor shall be selected by the Executive Board and shall serve in an advisory capacity without voting rights.

## Article X — Amendments

### Section 1 — Proposal

Amendments to these Bylaws may be proposed by any active member in writing to the Executive Board.

### Section 2 — Notice

Proposed amendments shall be distributed to the membership at least fourteen (14) days prior to the meeting at which they will be considered.

### Section 3 — Adoption

Amendments shall be adopted by a two-thirds (2/3) vote of the active members present at a meeting where a quorum is established.

## Article XI — Dissolution

In the event of dissolution of the Organization, all remaining funds and assets shall be distributed in accordance with University of North Carolina at Charlotte policies governing student organizations. No funds shall inure to the benefit of any individual member.

## Article XII — Parliamentary Authority

The rules contained in the current edition of *Robert's Rules of Order Newly Revised* shall govern the Organization in all cases to which they are applicable and in which they are not inconsistent with these Bylaws or any special rules the Organization may adopt.

## Article XIII — Ratification

These Bylaws shall become effective upon adoption by a two-thirds (2/3) vote of the founding members present at the organizational meeting.
