# Resolution of the Executive Board of the UNCC Pre-Law Club Organization (PCO)

## Resolution to Establish and Open a Trust Account

**Resolution No. 2026-001**

**Date: July 24, 2026**

---

## RECITALS

**WHEREAS**, the UNCC Pre-Law Club Organization ("PCO" or "the Organization") is a duly recognized student organization at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte; and

**WHEREAS**, the Executive Board of the Organization has determined that it is in the best interest of the Organization to establish a trust account for the purpose of holding, managing, and disbursing funds designated for organizational activities, scholarships, events, and special projects; and

**WHEREAS**, Article VII, Section 3 of the Bylaws of the Organization authorizes the Executive Board to establish trust accounts by resolution; and

**WHEREAS**, the Executive Board has determined that the establishment of such an account is necessary and proper for the effective financial management of the Organization;

---

## RESOLUTION

**NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED** that the Executive Board of the UNCC Pre-Law Club Organization hereby authorizes and directs the following:

### Section 1 — Establishment of Trust Account

The Organization shall open and establish a trust account (the "Trust Account") at a federally insured financial institution to be selected by the authorized officer(s) designated herein, in the name of the **UNCC Pre-Law Club Organization**.

### Section 2 — Purpose

The Trust Account shall be used for the following purposes:

1. Holding and safeguarding organizational funds designated for specific purposes.
2. Receiving membership dues, donations, fundraising proceeds, and other revenues.
3. Disbursing funds for authorized organizational expenses, events, programs, and activities.
4. Managing scholarship funds and special project allocations.
5. Any other lawful purpose consistent with the mission and Bylaws of the Organization.

### Section 3 — Authorized User

The Executive Board hereby designates the following individual as the **Authorized User** of the Trust Account, with full authority to open, manage, operate, and transact on behalf of the Organization:

> **Kareem Badran**
> **Title: Vice President**
> **UNCC Pre-Law Club Organization**

The Authorized User shall have the authority to:

- Open and establish the Trust Account at a qualifying financial institution.
- Make deposits into the Trust Account.
- Execute withdrawals and disbursements from the Trust Account in accordance with the Bylaws and this Resolution.
- Execute checks, electronic transfers, and other financial instruments drawn on the Trust Account.
- Access account statements, records, and online banking portals.
- Provide any documentation required by the financial institution for account opening and maintenance.
- Take any and all actions necessary and proper for the management of the Trust Account.

### Section 4 — Signatory Authority

The following individuals are authorized signatories on the Trust Account:

1. **Kareem Badran**, Vice President (Primary Authorized User)
2. **Mehdi Shawar**, President

For any single transaction exceeding **two hundred fifty dollars ($250.00)**, dual authorization from both signatories shall be required.

### Section 5 — Fiduciary Obligations

The Authorized User and all signatories shall manage the Trust Account in a fiduciary capacity and shall:

1. Use Trust Account funds solely for the purposes authorized by the Bylaws and the Executive Board.
2. Maintain accurate and complete records of all transactions.
3. Provide monthly account statements and transaction reports to the Executive Board.
4. Ensure that all disbursements are properly authorized and documented.
5. Cooperate with any audit or review of the Trust Account as directed by the Executive Board or the University.

### Section 6 — Reporting

The Authorized User shall provide a written report on the status of the Trust Account to the Executive Board at each regular Executive Board meeting, and to the general membership at least once per semester. The report shall include:

- Current account balance.
- Summary of deposits and withdrawals since the last report.
- Pending or anticipated transactions.
- Any discrepancies or irregularities.

### Section 7 — Compliance

The Trust Account shall be maintained in compliance with:

1. The Bylaws of the UNCC Pre-Law Club Organization.
2. All applicable policies and regulations of the University of North Carolina at Charlotte.
3. All applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations.

### Section 8 — Effective Date

This Resolution shall take effect immediately upon adoption by the Executive Board.
